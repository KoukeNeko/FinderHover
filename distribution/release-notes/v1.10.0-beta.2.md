## New and Improved

⚙️ Settings now show the result next to the controls: pick a section, see the popup for a sample file of that type, and turn its fields on or off beside it.

🔍 Search finds any field across all sections, and each section shows how many of its fields are on.

🖱️ Behavior shows the hover delay and how far the popup sits from the pointer over a Finder list, updating as you drag the sliders.

🖼️ Appearance shows the popup over a desktop background, so glass, blur, opacity, width and font size look the way they will.

🟨 New app icon.
